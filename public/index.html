    Patch Notes

    Version 1.6.1

    Scraping, Search & Rewards

    A focused quality-of-life update for managing large collections, earning gold from duplicates, and finding cards faster.

    • The Inventory now has a Scraping tab where duplicate cards can be converted into gold while keeping at least one copy and protecting cards needed by saved decks.
    • Scraping values are now visible in-game: Common cards give 1 gold, Rare 2, Legendary 3, Mythic 5, and Souvenir 10.
    • Scraping now asks for confirmation, shows a compact animated stack preview, and finishes with a gold-earned result modal.
    • Scrape all is available for the currently filtered duplicate list.
    • Collection, Deck Builder, Scraping, and Trade now support searching cards by name.
    • The old Type filter was removed from the Inventory tabs to make room for the faster card-name search.
    • The Trade card picker now uses card-name search in place of the selected-card text label, while the selected offer remains visible in the trade summary and card row.
    • Singleplayer victories now grant 10 gold, matching multiplayer wins.

    Version 1.6

    Embers, Exchange & Fair Play

    A visual refresh, smoother social features, stronger shop and collection screens, tournament quality-of-life upgrades, and a broad balance update for every way to play.

    • Main Menu refresh.
    • Choose a display name from your Profile without changing your Discord account or player ID. Open it with the pencil beside your name; names accept letters, numbers, and underscores only.
    • Trade has been rebuilt around a clearer offer workspace. Your collection is now a compact vertical list of five cards per page with numbered pagination, making offers easier to compare on desktop and mobile.
    • Trade controls and card panels now fit more cleanly inside their frame on desktop and phone layouts.
    • Card artwork in the collection zoom is larger and remains balanced on both phone and desktop layouts.
    • The Shop has a cleaner mobile-ready layout with a tighter wallet display, clearer pack details, QTY labels, pack odds, and a collection-focused dashboard.
    • The Inventory screen has a richer collection layout with stronger card presentation and better behavior across phone and desktop sizes.
    • Opening packs is more interactive: cards begin face down with expansion art, can be flipped manually or revealed all at once, and now hint rarity through the face-down border glow.
    • Card hover movement is now more expressive and consistent across Shop, Inventory, deck views, and pack opening without stretching the card art.
    • Tournament brackets now open from the ? button on tournament cards into a dedicated responsive modal.
    • On phones, tournament rounds use a compact dropdown, match cards are smaller, the bracket scrolls vertically, and accidental text selection or sideways dragging is reduced.
    • On desktop, the tournament podium stays visible beside the bracket and the bracket scrollbar has matching styling.
    • Active tournaments automatically focus the round currently in progress. Registered players see their own relevant match, status, and essential details first; completed brackets stop refreshing unnecessarily.
    • Tournament readiness is more reliable: players who arrive are tracked, one-sided no-shows advance the waiting player, and a match with no arrivals no longer blocks the bracket.
    • In every mode except Singleplayer, the player going second receives Mana Spark: a 0-cost Special card that grants 1 temporary Mana for the current turn.
    • Only one Charge minion can be played to a side of the board at once.
    • Overseer now costs 3 Mana and is a 4/6. On its first death it deals 20 damage to all minions, then returns with 1 Health; the explosion cannot trigger again.
    • Fanex now steals a random minion from the enemy board and immediately deploys it on your side.
    • New achievements have been added for reaching Top 5, reaching Top 1, completing the Base collection, and completing the Expansion 1 collection. Reaching Top 1 also unlocks a new title.
    • Souvenir cards are now available as a new 0.6% rarity tier.
    • Base packs cost 15 gold and Expansion 1 packs cost 25 gold.
    • General balance pass across Base, Expansion 1, and The Gates. Most affected cards gained 1-3 Health, alongside targeted Mana, Attack, and effect adjustments.

    Version 1.5.4

    PWA & Tournament Stability

    Arcana is easier to install, more resilient during tournament starts, and clearer at a glance.

    • Install Arcana TCG as an app on compatible desktop and mobile browsers. An in-game install notice appears in the main menu when the browser confirms it is available.
    • The PWA cache now updates safely between releases without caching login, Discord Activity, profile, match, or other live API requests.
    • Tournament matches now start through a protected queue, preventing a rush of bracket pairings from loading the server at once. Duplicate starts, failed preparations, and disconnects are handled safely.
    • The tournament lobby now tells both players when a match is being prepared and preserves a clear re-entry path if preparation cannot finish.
    • Mythic Lolflame now deals 1 damage to every enemy minion whenever it attacks, including when it attacks the opposing hero. Deck lists now show Charge, Taunt, and Divine Shield beside card costs.
    • Updated the main-menu music rotation with MainMenu1 and removed the retired Board4 track from the board rotation.
    • Mobile audio now retries during touch input and offers an Enable sound action whenever the browser blocks playback after login.

    Version 1.5.3

    Iron Watch: TheUnchained

    Stage 2 is now open, bringing a new boss encounter built around the Shield Trial.

    • Challenge TheUnchained in Iron Watch. The boss has 30 Health, follows normal Mana progression, and unleashes its 0/20 encounter-only Mythic after building enough Mana.
    • The Shield Trial returns whenever TheUnchained enters the board or survives to another turn. Block incoming arrows with the shield to avoid damage.
    • The trial grows through long battles: more arrows, faster patterns, longer sequences, mobile swipe controls, and hit or block sound feedback.
    • Defeat TheUnchained to earn 250 gold. This campaign reward is available once per account and its claimed state is visible from the stage reward panel.
    • Earn the new Unchained Conqueror achievement and its decorative profile badge for defeating the boss.
    • Targeted spells now hide the hand on mobile while selected or dragged, keeping hero and board targets clear without breaking the hand swipe gesture.

    • Hero healing is now capped at maximum Health, while minion healing can still exceed maximum Health. Quick Bandage and Greater Blessing now state this clearly.

    • Fanex is a Mythic 6 Mana 6/10. On play, it steals a random minion from the enemy board and deploys it to your board.

    • Zoblezar no longer has Divine Shield or draws cards on death. The first time it dies, it revives as a 5/10.
    • Zugzwang is now an 8/7 Charge minion. Its former three-card draw is replaced with a Minor Spark added to your hand at the start of every one of your turns while it survives.
    • Humph is now a 7 Mana 8/10 without Divine Shield. When it dies, every remaining minion on both boards returns to its owner's deck.
    • GoldenWarerita now transforms directly into a normal 1/5 Warerita when it dies, preserving its position on the board instead of creating a separate summon.
    • Moonhammer changed from 3/9 to 4/8. Red gained 1 Health, becoming 0/9. Tucuquere is now 7/3, and its display name is capitalized.
    • Chiorico is now a 5 Mana 6/7 without Charge. Mamaluteo is now a 7 Mana 5/7. These changes give their disruptive effects more appropriate timing and survivability.
    • Base-card balance: Dezadin costs 8; Juniiya is 7 Mana 6/5; Lifelinker costs 9; the Mythic Lolflame is 3 Mana 3/7 while the common version is 4 Mana 2/6; Multimaker is 7 Mana 1/6; PrinceMVC is 3/10; Ranger costs 9; and Rin is 7 Mana 5/6.
    • Collection and Deck Builder can now sort cards by Mana, Attack, or Health in either direction.
    • Hero Health and Mana values have sharper, higher-contrast presentation across desktop and mobile. Impact sounds now vary slightly in pitch, and board music stops immediately when a match result appears.

    Legal Notice

    How to Play

    Win the Duel

    Reduce the opposing hero from 30 Health to 0. You can also win when your opponent surrenders. If both heroes reach 0 Health at once, the match is a draw.

    Rounds and Mana

    • Rounds are global: Player A acts, Player B acts, then the next round begins.
    • At the start of your turn, gain one maximum Mana up to 10, refill your Mana, draw one card, and ready your minions.
    • In multiplayer, each turn has a 40-second limit. Opening hands contain 3 cards for the first player and 4 for the second, then the first turn draws as normal.
    • Your hand holds up to 10 cards. Drawing with a full hand burns the drawn card; an empty deck simply cannot draw.

    Cards and Combat

    Play cards from your hand by paying the blue Mana crystal shown in their top corner. Minions enter your board. Spells resolve their written effect and then leave play.

    • Minions normally cannot attack until their next turn. Select or drag a ready minion onto an enemy minion or hero to attack.
    • Minion combat is mutual: both minions deal their Attack to each other at the same time.
    • Direct attacks damage the enemy hero, but Taunt minions must be attacked first.

    Board Rules

    • You may normally control up to 4 minions. Summoning effects can create one extra minion, for a maximum of 5.
    • You may have up to 2 Taunt minions and up to 1 Charge minion on your board.

    Card Legend

    3Mana cost - paid to play the card.
    4Attack - damage dealt by a minion.
    5Health - a minion is destroyed at 0.
    Taunt - enemies must attack this before other targets.
    ϟCharge - can attack on the turn it is played.
    ✦Divine Shield - prevents the first damage it would take, then breaks.

    Card borders indicate rarity: Common, Rare, Legendary, and Mythic. Rarity does not change combat rules by itself.

    Status Legends

    WWeakened - loses the listed Attack for its duration.
    FFrozen - cannot attack for its duration.
    XSilenced - its abilities and keywords are removed permanently.
    PPoisoned - minions and heroes take the listed damage at the start of their turn. Reapplying Poison refreshes it instead of stacking damage.
    !Marked - the next damage it takes is increased by the listed amount, then the mark is removed.
    BBurning - minions and heroes take the listed damage at the start of their turn. More Burning adds damage and duration.
    DDrunk - attacks a random minion on either side instead of the chosen target.

    Build a Deck

    Every deck has exactly 20 cards. Common and Rare cards allow up to 2 copies of the same card. Legendary and Mythic cards allow 1 copy each; a deck can contain up to 3 Legendary cards and 1 Mythic card in total.

    Useful Controls

    Click a minion card to play it quickly, or drag it from your hand to the board. Click or drag a ready minion toward its target to attack. The small smiling button beside End Turn opens emotes for your opponent.
